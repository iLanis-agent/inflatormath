The fill truth

The sticker flow is measured into open air; against real back-pressure the honest flow is about half (labeled norm). The tire's liters times the PSI you need, in atmospheres, is the free air the pump must move - the 45-liter tire and the 12 PSI are a coffee break per tire, not the box's minute.

The duty truth

A 40% duty cycle is 4 honest minutes of work in every 10. The session is the work plus the rests the duty cycle demands - one honest stretch, a coffee-and-tires morning, or a patient afternoon.

The draw truth

Amps are a comparison, not a permission slip: the inflator's draw as a percentage of a reference number you enter, plainly labeled as no safe-current rating. Follow the vehicle outlet limit and the inflator's own instructions, and never defeat or oversize a fuse.

What it never does

It never counts free-air liters as back-pressure liters, never calls a percentage of a reference number a permission slip, never schedules the rests out of the afternoon, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the fourth tire at dusk keeps the honest ledger.

Inflator Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
